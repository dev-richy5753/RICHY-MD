GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

What does the app do?

Enter your WhatsApp number, receive a linking code, then monitor connection health, uptime, and pairing activity.

How do I approve a pairing?

Request a code for a number you own, then follow WhatsApp’s device-linking flow in your own WhatsApp app. Never share a pairing code with anyone else.

Does RICHY MD read my messages for analytics?

No. The dashboard analytics use connection and pairing events; they do not include message contents.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The app is designed for mobile screens and can be installed from a supported browser or as an Android APK.
